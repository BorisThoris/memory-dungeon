Memory Dungeon privacy policy

Last updated 31 October 2026. This covers the game on the web and the Android app, which opens the same web game.

No account, no tracking

Memory Dungeon has no sign-in and no accounts. It shows no ads, uses no advertising ID, and runs no analytics or tracking.

Your saves stay on your device

Your runs, records, collection, settings and progress are stored in your browser's or the app's local storage on your device. They are not sent anywhere. Clearing the app's data, or the site's data in your browser, deletes them.

The global record

The main menu shows one global record: the best score anyone has set, with the nickname they chose. When a run beats it, the game offers to put your score on it under a nickname you type. This is optional. If you agree, the nickname and score are sent to the game's record service and shown publicly to every player until someone beats them. Only the current record is kept; a beaten record is replaced and not stored.

To stop a submission being abused, the record service keeps the IP address it came from, with a count, for up to one hour, and then deletes it. The IP address is used for nothing else and is not shared.

Who sees your data

Nobody else gets it. The data above is not sold or shared with third parties. The game and the record service are hosted on Cloudflare, which processes the requests to serve them. All of it is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over.

Removing your nickname

To have your nickname taken off the global record, or with any question about this policy, write to modaxxx009@yahoo.com.

Changes

If the game starts keeping or sending anything else, this page will say so before it does.
