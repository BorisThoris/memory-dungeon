# Camp economy (historical rules 58)

Current solo endless runs use rules 59: randomized stackable perks, mass-card fields, automated harvest and meteors. See [Endless god runs](ENDLESS_GOD_RUNS.md). This document preserves the preceding three-rank economy for legacy runs and practice fixtures.

Clears select the next arena from the run seed automatically. The reward beat leads directly into the next floor; every third clear opens camp. Its Continue button and Escape both enter the selected arena. Pass-and-play skips camp.

Gold is the only shop resource. Essence drops, focus purchases and bottled reactions are removed from new runs. The card elements and their match reactions remain part of play. Historical rules 55–57 keep their economy functions for deterministic replay; they are not the current shop.

| Upgrade | Rank 1 / 2 / 3 effect | Gold per rank |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Long Look | +1 / +2 / +3 seconds to study every floor | 8 / 14 / 20 |
| Deep Pockets | 5 / 6 / 7 miss capacity; restores 1 miss on purchase | 8 / 14 / 20 |
| Gilded Chain | 2 / 3 / 4 gold each fifth consecutive match | 6 / 11 / 16 |

Misses still expire three floors after their grant. Deep Pockets expands capacity, not lifetime. Combo gold pays even if the miss bank is full. Each upgrade caps at rank 3 and appears once in the relic inventory; its purchase count stores its rank.

Emergency supplies compete with investment: a miss costs 4 gold (+2 per previous purchase), a peek costs 3 (+1), and a bomb costs 4 (+2). A full bank cannot buy a miss. All six products are present at every camp, making it possible to plan a build and save toward the next rank. Gold and upgrades last only for this run.

The hook is an understandable choice between immediate help and a stronger run: buy study time to remember more, capacity to survive mistakes, or combo income to fund future upgrades. No rerolls, random purchase outcomes, or additional currency are involved. Live descriptions show the current and next effect, rank, price and exact gold shortfall. Inventory shows purchased ranks between camps.

Verification covers purchase prices, rank caps, effects on the real timer/miss budget, combo payouts, persistence across floors, seeded arena variation, no elemental purchases/drops, complete run simulations, authored practice rooms, and isolated browser keyboard/accessibility/mobile checks.
