## Problem

## Target experience
A single schema bump adds: `unlocks.endless`, `unlocks.depths` (highest), `unlocks.feats[]`, `unlocks.blueprints[]`, deepest act and tier, Warden clears, best Depth. Old saves normalise with defaults; crash logs stay excluded from cloud.
